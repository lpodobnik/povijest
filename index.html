The Ancient World

One connected story in 18 chapters, from the first upright walkers to the fall of Rome. Egypt runs through it from the green Sahara to Cleopatra.

  1. Story mode tells it step by step.
  2. The map is a time machine. Drag the time bar or press ▶ and watch ice sheets melt, people spread, deserts dry and empires grow. Click any place for a picture.
  3. Every chapter asks why. Beside each story you will find what caused it and what it led to. Click a cause to jump back to where it began.
  4. The bottom timeline shows "meanwhile". Each row is a part of the world, so you can see what was happening everywhere at the same moment.
